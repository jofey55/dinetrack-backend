## Design Improvements for Your Inventory Tool

To modernize your inventory tracking tool and ensure visual alignment with Zawadi Center, here’s a guide on color usage, layout, and branding:

### 1. **Brand Colors & Visual Theme**

- **Primary Colors (From Zawadi Center):**
  - Blue: `#3b85db` (vibrant accent)
  - Deep Blue: `#3349c0`
  - Charcoal: `#2b2a35` (main background/headers)
  - Dark Slate: `#1f2b3f`
  - Soft Gray: `#626272`
  - Bright Yellow: `#ffb30b` (secondary highlight)
  - White: `#ffffff` (background, sections)

- **Usage Suggestion:**
  - Use deep blue (`#3349c0`) for major headers and navigation backgrounds.
  - Accent buttons and highlights with blue (`#3b85db`) and yellow (`#ffb30b`) for vibrancy.
  - Apply white or soft gray for content areas to keep things readable and spacious[1].

### 2. **Logo Placement**

- Place the Zawadi Center logo (SVG: `/assets/images/softr_logo/softr_logo_icon_only.svg`) at the top left of the site’s header, matching the original site.
- Use a clean header bar with a deep blue or charcoal background for strong contrast[1].

### 3. **Modern Layout & Organization**

- **Sidebar Navigation:**  
  Collapse and expand sections/subsections in a left-side panel with high contrast headers and clear icons.
- **Cards or Rows for Items:**  
  Each inventory item appears as a card or row with:
  - Name, quantity, minimum threshold
  - Inline “Order Now” button (colored in blue/yellow), and quantity input
- **Colorful Indicators:**  
  Use colored badges or icons to flag low stock (e.g., yellow circles for needs ordering)
- **Subsections:**  
  Indent or nest with lighter blues or drop-shadowed cards—allows easy visual tracking of nested groups while keeping it tidy.

### 4. **Enhanced Visual Features**

- **Consistent Font & Spacing:**  
  Use a modern, sans-serif font and generous spacing/padding for clarity.
- **Sticky Header:**  
  Keep header navigation and logo visible on scroll.
- **Card Effects:**  
  Add subtle shadows/hover effects on cards and buttons to give a lively, tactile feel.
- **Animated Buttons:**  
  “Order Now” buttons animate slightly on hover/click for feedback and engagement.
- **Warm Highlighting:**  
  Use bright yellow for important actions (like order notifications) to catch the user’s eye without overwhelming the interface.

### 5. **Result: Clean, Branded, Colorful UI**

Your redesigned tool will combine function and a modern, colorful experience by:

- Featuring Zawadi Center’s logo and core colors for instant brand recognition.
- Utilizing a deep blue/charcoal palette with bright accent colors for navigation and alerts.
- Organizing sections with clear, collapsible nesting for a tidy yet detailed overview.
- Highlighting important actions (ordering, low stock) with colorful, easy-to-spot cues.

By following these guidelines, your inventory tool will not only “look better” and more modern but will also integrate seamlessly with Zawadi Center’s established visual identity[1].
